About Krishi Setu

Who we are, and why this exists.

Krishi Setu is a demonstration agricultural platform built around one idea: connect the farmer directly with the market, the buyer, the logistics and the money — without losing transparency at any step.

Mission

Farm. Connect. Prosper.

Most farmers face the same set of problems: prices are opaque, buyers are far away, logistics are costly and payments are slow. Krishi Setu makes each of those steps visible and comparable.

The problem

A fragmented chain costs farmers twice.

Fragmented price information across mandis and channels. Long intermediary chains that reduce the farmer's share. Post-harvest losses from poor storage and logistics planning. Slow, unclear settlement after the sale.

The solution

One workspace from inventory to settlement.

Market intelligence, buyer discovery, logistics planning and financial visibility in a single connected flow. The same produce data stays consistent from the inventory batch to the final transaction.

Who it connects

Farmers · Buyers · Logistics · Finance

👨‍🌾

Farmers & FPOs

Track inventory, publish lots, compare offers and see true take-home value before selling.

🏪

Institutional buyers

Browse verified lots, inspect quality, post RFQs and manage orders with gate-QC flows.

🚚

Logistics partners

Transport options compared by speed, capacity, cost and spoilage risk for fresh produce.

🏦

Finance & storage

Warehouse financing, escrow settlement and payment rails built around the sale lifecycle.
