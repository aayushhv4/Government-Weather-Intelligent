The farmer

Every harvested batch, tracked from day one.

Log crop, variety, harvest date, storage and moisture. AI freshness scoring and shelf-life prediction tell you which batch to sell first — before quality drops.

The same batch then flows consistently into publishing, offers, net-realisation and settlement.

Krishi Setu

Market, buyers and logistics — connected.

Publish produce lots with one click from inventory. Buyers see freshness, storage and trust ratings. Offers arrive with pickup and payment terms — compare by net realisation, not headline price.

Finance

Know your true take-home before you sell.

Profit calculators and the net-realisation engine compare mandi, direct buyer and quick-commerce channels — transport, cess and fees deducted, so the number you see is the number you keep.

Settlement runs through an escrow rail and releases on delivery approval.

Logistics

From farm gate to buyer — with a plan.

Compare transport options by speed, capacity, cost and spoilage risk. Pair perishable batches with cold-chain or reefers, and track the journey from dispatch to delivery.
