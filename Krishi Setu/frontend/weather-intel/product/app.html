Scientific Architecture

Downscaling Methodology

The 8-step agro-meteorological downscaling pipeline from Block bulletin to farm-level decision support.

1

Block Forecast Ingestion

Receives coarse daily maximum temperature (B_t) and rainfall for the entire administrative Block.

2

Geospatial Feature Extraction

Extracts localized elevation (z_p), slope, aspect, land cover, and NDVI for each Gram Panchayat.

3

Named Physical Baselines

Computes B1 (uniform Block copy) and B2 (environmental lapse rate: -6.5 °C/km) to benchmark performance.

4

Random Forest Residual Correction

Applies trained residual model: T_p = B_t + f_T(X_p), capturing non-linear terrain thermal influences.

5

Mean-Preserving Reconciliation

Performs area-weighted adjustment: T*_p = T_p + (B_t − Σ w_p · T_p) to guarantee block energy conservation.

6

Indicative Uncertainty Bounds

Calculates empirical P10–P90 residual quantiles per Panchayat to communicate forecast uncertainty honestly.

7

Hindcast Validation

Evaluates MAE, RMSE, and Skill Score against independent temporal splits (2023–2024 test window).

8

Weather → Action Advisory Chain

Maps downscaled temperature to crop vulnerability thresholds, delivering concrete operational field directives.
